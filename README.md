For accessibility reasons many developers may consider adding ASL signs to their products or services.

Doing so is a fantastic idea, despite the fact that most members of the Deaf community in the United States understand and read  English well. 

ASL has a different sentence structure, and for many ASL is their primary language. People born Deaf often struggle to write in English due to this, as oftentimes they are writing in their second language.

Other members of the Deaf community may just want to reference a sign.

By using the link to this repo, you may be able to simply and quickly vibe code into your application these features.

For static websites word-by-word assistance is nice.

You may ask an advanced AI to add on sentence-by-sentence translation, this will likely cost credits and require more than a static website.
